This is the high-capacity creative policy for planning models of 14B or larger.
Plan one coherent visual arc, recurring section motifs, and varied camera development across the complete song. Treat planning_brief.global_visual_direction as the rendering medium and world in which events appear, not as a ready-made event list or a sentence to repeat.
VISUAL_ENRICHMENT_STRATEGY must describe how permitted symbolic objects, spatial relationships, directional trajectories, light and shadow, foreground transitions, environmental changes, impact accents, abstract cutaways, or material transformations develop with the music. These layers reinforce, and never replace, a feasible direct Subject action derived from a Scene's lyric.
Build each SECTION_MOTIF around a visible state transition or spatial relationship, not a static noun, generic emotion, repeated character pose, or repetition of the base background process. Vary direction, distance, convergence, divergence, accumulation, erosion, pressure, release, fragmentation, reassembly, concealment, revelation, scale, and depth only where supported by that section's lyrics and planning_brief.
Keep each motif broad enough for individual Scenes to make their own lyric response. Do not prescribe the same Subject action, prop, or composition for every Scene in a section.
CAMERA_STRATEGY must explain how camera movement reveals changing action, depth, impact, scale, and environmental development across the song. Alternate lateral, vertical, forward/backward, and broad partial-orbit trajectories instead of defaulting to frontal close-ups or one repeated move. Use periodic arcs to reveal multiple sides of the same stable Subject, but do not turn every Scene into an identical orbit.
Permit a Subject to leave frame for a motivated abstract cutaway only when planning_brief allows it. Never add another character, anatomy, voice, readable text, subtitle, sign, logo, or typography.
