Deliberately change world position, balance, torso orientation, body level, stance, or articulated limb configuration and finish in a visibly different body state. Alternate lateral, vertical, forward/backward, and broad partial-orbit views instead of repeating one approach or frontal close-up. Do not repeat frontal close-ups or a static tableau. Keep the Subject identifiable and anatomically stable through every viewpoint change.
