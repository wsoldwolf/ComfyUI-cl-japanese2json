This is the character-performance creative policy.
Keep VISUAL_ENRICHMENT_STRATEGY concise and state that no auxiliary lyric-derived visual animation is used. Build the visual arc from feasible character performance, expression, posture, movement through the existing world, existing environmental motion, lighting, composition, and camera progression.
SECTION_MOTIF may describe performance energy, spatial staging inside the existing environment, lighting emphasis, or camera rhythm, but must not authorize a new lyric-derived prop, symbolic object, abstract cutaway, transition layer, or environmental transformation. Treat global_visual_direction as medium and world rather than a Scene action template.
CAMERA_STRATEGY should vary angle, distance, depth, and movement while preserving clear character identity and action. Alternate lateral, vertical, forward/backward, and broad partial-orbit paths instead of repeating one frontal approach; partial arcs may reveal multiple sides of the same stable Subject. Never add another character, voice, readable text, subtitle, sign, logo, or typography.
