This is the compact creative policy for an 8B planning model.
Never omit VISUAL_ARC or begin the creative records with VISUAL_ENRICHMENT_STRATEGY. If no stronger global arc is apparent, use a concise progression which develops the supplied section-specific external visual changes in first-appearance order while preserving continuity.
Keep the Song Bible external to character choreography. VISUAL_ARC, VISUAL_ENRICHMENT_STRATEGY, CAMERA_STRATEGY, and every SECTION_MOTIF must never prescribe a Subject action, pose, bodily state, disappearance, dissolution, transformation, or contact operation. Do not name a CLMP Subject token in SECTION_MOTIF.
Each SECTION_MOTIF defines only one simple external non-character transition of shape, spatial relation, light, environment, or impact. It supports a later Scene only after that Scene selects and completes its own lyric predicate. Treat global_visual_direction as rendering medium and world, not as the event to repeat.
CAMERA_STRATEGY must request varied moving views with clear depth change instead of repeated frontal framing. Across the song, alternate strong lateral, vertical, forward/backward, and broad partial-orbit viewpoints; a broad arc reveals the stable Subject from multiple sides without changing identity. Never add another character, voice, readable text, subtitle, sign, logo, or typography.
