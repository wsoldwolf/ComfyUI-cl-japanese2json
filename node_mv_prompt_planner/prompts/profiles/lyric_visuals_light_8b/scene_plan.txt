1. LYRIC FIRST. When locked_lyric_action_blueprint is present, do not reinterpret the lyric: copy its LYRIC_RESPONSE values, keep its composition_requirement recognizable in every Shot, and make the camera and environment reveal its chronological_actions and visible_result. Choose the earliest complete depictable predicate; never replace it with a later refrain, repeated fragment, uppercase emphasis, or shorter line. Ignore an active_section_motif that conflicts with the selected predicate. Make the dominant ACTION perform the selected predicate's actual physical head verb on its explicit target. A different operation on the same target is invalid. Use direct_subject_action whenever an active Subject can perform the verb without violating planning_brief; otherwise use direct_object_action, then spatial_metaphor.
2. OBSERVABLE MECHANICS. ROLE LOCK: keep produced content or marks separate from the target substrate. The target must already exist as a recognizable solid object or surface in COMPOSITION; describe its usable face, thickness, edges, orientation, depth, and the same non-photoreal medium or line quality required by global_visual_direction. It is where the action occurs, never a target-like shape, an outline drawn in empty space, a floating representation, or detached glyph-shaped content. For a direct contact action, use two to four adjacent ACTION lines for PREPARE, exact CONTACT POINT, verb-specific PATH OR REPETITION with suitable FORCE, and visible RESULT with RELEASE. For deliberate mark-making, give the target a broad stable face, contact it with a permitted fingertip or held tool, stabilize the torso and upper arm, use several controlled non-uniform hand, wrist, or finger paths, accumulate only isolated non-linguistic short scratches or grooves with irregular length, direction, curvature, and spacing on or in that same face following its perspective, then separate the effector and reveal the complete changed target. In creative fields never call the residue a name, text, word, letter, character, glyph, inscription, symbol, caption, subtitle, logo, or typography. Never form a baseline, horizontal or vertical row, repeated glyph-like pattern, word spacing, mirrored lettering, or reflected lettering. Never replace mark-making with striking, crushing, stepping on, broad sweeping, one whole-arm impact, readable text, or floating marks. Section intensity may change speed or scale only after the operation is fixed. Never remove, dissolve, transform, or hide a retained Subject property.
3. SUBJECT MOTION AND FORM. Obey subject_motion_contract. When an ACTION names a Subject, passive floating, bobbing, swaying, expression, gaze, mouth, hair, clothing, effects, or camera movement does not count. Use at least the required number of deliberate chronological body phases, ending with changed balance, torso direction, body level, stance, world position, or limb configuration. Keep the Subject visually substantial: preserve the same head, neck, ribcage, pelvis, limbs, clothing layers, overlap, foreshortening, contour turns, and depth separation in the requested medium. Rough marks must not collapse into a flat icon, generic cheap illustration, mascot, or featureless silhouette.
4. MOVING CAMERA. Obey every binding required_camera_sequence, including instrumental, silent, and short Scenes. A lyric Scene of 10 seconds or longer uses exactly two Shots; every other Scene uses exactly one Shot. Copy each entry's type, amplitude, and speed exactly, then write a concrete Japanese path that realizes its spatial_goal around the current action. A required broad arc must physically travel around the same stable Subject and name at least two different zones among front, side, and rear, not merely approach, move laterally, or hold one angle. Keep the complete Subject, recognizable target, contact point, foreground depth anchor, and distant background anchor readable across the viewpoint change. Every CAMERA sentence must name its start view, travel path, foreground/background parallax, and different end view. A generic approach, retreat, orbit, or follow statement is invalid. Do not use tracking unless the Subject or main target changes world position.
5. AUXILIARY LAST. Every Scene must contain exactly one AUX_VISUAL line across all Shots. Put it inside exactly one Shot after its final ACTION and before ENVIRONMENT. Use the exact required_kind supplied by auxiliary_visual_contract. It is one external non-character visible change with initial state, motion, and result. It may reinforce only after the selected lyric action is complete and never replaces that action, target, contact, result, camera path, or retained Subject property. Do not add another character, anatomy, voice, readable word, subtitle, sign, logo, or typography.
